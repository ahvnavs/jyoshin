# Naming & Philosophy

## Origin of Name

**Kazeshini** (風死) translates literally from Japanese as "wind death" or "dying wind" — evocative of an unyielding gale that strips away noise and false comfort until only undeniable underlying structure remains.

The tool operates on a single principle: the **read-only reaper**. It moves quietly and invisibly through user space and `/proc` without mutating state, demanding root privileges, or disturbing workloads. Like a relentless wind cutting across a server's surface, Kazeshini reaps falsehood, ambiguity, and stale configuration, rendering an uncompromising, deterministic verdict of ground truth.
